ernel helps any model continuously improve by extracting and dreaming on the context of any user, task, or tenant, and putting it on our vector-graph database. This is then brought to the model by injecting tokens into its context in real time.

What we do

Private context that stays private.
Hush computes over selected private context locally or behind an encrypted boundary and returns the decision, summary or capability the model needs — not the underlying record.
One approved memory, across models.
Any assistant can propose a fact or preference. Hush keeps the canonical memory user-owned so continuity follows you across models and harnesses.
Authority without handing over secrets.
Agents request narrow capabilities. Hush checks policy, binds approval, keeps credentials outside model context and records what actually happened.

Hush is being built local-first and provider-independent: the model-facing surface is intentionally smaller than the private state behind it.
