Know what you’re using.

Hush’s current access, responsibilities and product limits.

CURRENT ACCESS

Free to explore.

The website and browser workspace are available to explore without a Hush account or paid subscription. No hosted account, paid plan or subscription purchase is offered here. The local runtime and companion surfaces have separate setup and release requirements.

YOUR WORKSPACE

Keep the keys to your context.

You manage your passphrase, local browser storage and encrypted backups. There is no recovery service or password reset. Review text before copying it to an AI provider, and use only information you are authorized to store or share. Provider terms and privacy policies continue to apply.

EARLY ACCESS LIMITS

Evaluate before depending on it.

Features and interfaces can change. Hush does not guarantee complete detection of sensitive content, universal leakage prevention, production suitability for every workflow or the behavior of external AI providers. The browser app does not enforce permissions on another app. The local runtime has defined security and release limits.
