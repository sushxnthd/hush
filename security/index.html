Security boundary

Privacy needs a clear boundary.

Hush is an engineering alpha. Its protections have a defined scope and unfinished release gates.

What the local core is designed to do.

  • Keep encrypted private context behind a local computation boundary.
  • Return bounded results and account for repeated explicit disclosures.
  • Require scoped authority and approvals for supported actions.
  • Keep reusable credentials outside supported model-facing action paths.
  • Record auditable action receipts.

What the evidence does not establish.

Published evaluations are bounded experiments. They do not demonstrate universal security, full prevention of information leakage, or independent third-party certification. Results on Hush-generated traces and synthetic workloads do not establish general model behavior.

Outside the current guarantee.

Compromised hosts, bypassing Hush, timing and network metadata, covert channels, unsupported client behavior and arbitrary end-to-end semantic leakage remain important limits. OS-backed key handling, broad protocol compatibility and consumer onboarding are still being developed.

Report a security issue.

Follow the repository security policy for current reporting options. Do not put credentials, exploit-sensitive details or private records in public issues.
