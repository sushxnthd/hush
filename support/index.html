Where are my notes stored?

Your browser workspace is saved as encrypted ciphertext in this browser’s IndexedDB. It is separate from the local runtime. No Hush cloud account or automatic synchronization is involved.

What if I forget my passphrase?

Hush cannot reset it or decrypt your workspace for you. Keep your passphrase safely and export an encrypted backup. Clearing browser data can remove your saved workspace.

Does Hush connect automatically to ChatGPT or Claude?

The browser workspace prepares reviewed text for manual copy and paste. It does not log into, monitor or control another AI app. Local MCP integration is a separate runtime setup.

What happens when I forget a memory?

It is removed from this workspace and the prepared context is cleared. Hush cannot delete copies already pasted into a provider or retained in a previously exported backup.

Why is the workspace locked or saving blocked?

It locks after 15 minutes of inactivity. A save conflict means another tab changed the same workspace; lock and unlock to load the latest version. If storage is unavailable, use a normal browser window or sample mode.

How do I restore a backup?

Choose your encrypted backup JSON file, confirm replacement if a workspace exists, and enter the original passphrase. Export your current workspace before restoring.

Why won’t the local runtime start?

Check Node.js 22+, an available loopback port and a working OS keystore. Linux needs Secret Service and secret-tool. See the repository diagnostics; do not silently bypass a production security failure.

Can I use Hush as production security infrastructure?

The browser workspace is early access and the local runtime is a release candidate. Use the published scope and release evidence to assess suitability. Independent review and broad distribution are still pending.

Report a bug or ask a question.

Include the page or feature, browser or Node version, expected behavior and steps to reproduce. Keep private records, passphrases and credentials out of public issues.
