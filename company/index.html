WHY HUSH

AI is getting personal.
The control layer should be yours.

Hush is building the user-owned context and permission layer for a world where one person uses many AI systems.

THE PROBLEM

Personalization keeps asking for a copy.

Useful assistants need context. Today, people repeatedly describe their preferences, projects and private circumstances inside separate chat products. Continuity is fragmented, and sharing more context often means giving another provider another copy.

As assistants gain the ability to act, the question expands: not just “what does it know?”, but “what is it allowed to do?”

THE PRODUCT THESIS

Keep state with the user.
Release the useful part.

Hush puts context and authority behind one user-controlled boundary. The browser workspace is the first accessible surface: private capture, approved memory and deliberate sharing. The local runtime provides the deeper boundary: bounded private computation, reconstruction accounting, supported secretless actions and signed receipts.

The long-term direction is one portable relationship with your context across AI providers. Current shipping scope and remaining release gates are explicit.
