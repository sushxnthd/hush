Latest validation: independent AgentLeak detector

What changed, and what the change actually proves.

A concise record of shipped research, product primitives and evidence.

October 2026

  1. Oct 5 · validation

    Independent AgentLeak regression passed on production ActionBroker traces.

    Across 50 paired synthetic traces, Hush preserved 100% task success with 0% credential-canary leakage and 0% any-finding rate; the naive path leaked in 100% of trials.

  2. Oct 5 · connector compute

    Bounded computation reached structured connector fields.

    160 controlled tasks across Gmail, Calendar, Drive and GitHub reached 160/160 correct decisions with zero exact benchmark-secret exposures and zero connector-path exposures.

  3. Oct 5 · research

    Lineage-aware local representation became freeze-ready.

    On 67 development scenarios, completeness was 0.9701 vs 0.9754 semantic-only while protected-context violation fell from 0.9226 to 0.7712. Paired leak-free outcomes were 7–0; confirmatory evaluation remains outstanding.

  4. Oct 5 · product

    v1.2 acceptance contract became canonical.

    The next release is defined by explicit product, privacy, authority, integration and validation gates instead of a loose feature list.

  5. Earlier · privacy

    Persistent reconstruction accounting closed task-reset and sink-rotation attacks.

    Disclosure accounting now persists across trajectories, agents, destinations and restarts.

Current status

v0.9 alpha. Hush is now a personal trust layer, not only a private-computation kernel.

The repository includes encrypted context, semantic bounded queries over structured connector fields, cumulative reconstruction accounting, approval-gated shared memory, privacy-aware routing, secretless action brokerage, ciphertext-only sync and browser/desktop/mobile client work.
