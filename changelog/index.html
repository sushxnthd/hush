Latest: research + v1.2 convergence

What changed, and what the change actually proves.

A concise record of shipped research, product primitives, failures and claim boundaries.

October 2026

  1. Oct 5 · research

    Scientific Superiority Protocol v1 passed all preregistered gates.

    Across 4,000 paired bounded-personalization tasks, Hush matched the oracle on 100% of tasks while exposing 0.000 exact private input attributes per task. Coarse minimization reached 84.125% oracle agreement while exposing 0.938 exact private attributes per task. The same protocol also recorded 0% exact recovery under the tested Hush reconstruction conditions versus 100% for the unprotected control.

  2. Oct 5 · external holdout

    AgentCIBench confirmatory v1 passed all eight frozen scientific gates.

    On the frozen 50-case independent benchmark holdout, Hush preserved 95.07% mean completeness while reducing mean exact protected-context violation from 0.9410 to 0.7960 versus the matched semantic-only retriever. Paired leak-free wins were 6–0 with p = 0.03125.

  3. Oct 5 · validation

    Independent AgentLeak detector found zero credential-canary leakage on the secretless-action path.

    Across 50 paired production-ActionBroker traces, Hush preserved 100% task success with 0% credential-canary leakage; the naive comparator exposed the canary in every paired trial.

  4. Oct 5 · development

    Lineage-aware local representation became freeze-ready, but remains development-only.

    Across 67 development scenarios, completeness was 0.9701 versus 0.9754 semantic-only while protected-context violation fell from 0.9226 to 0.7712. Paired leak-free outcomes were 7–0; p = 0.015625. A fresh untouched confirmation is still required.

  5. Oct 5 · connector compute

    Bounded computation reached structured connector fields.

    160 controlled tasks across Gmail, Calendar, Drive and GitHub reached 160/160 correct bounded decisions with zero exact benchmark-secret exposures and zero connector-path exposures.

  6. Oct 5 · product

    The v1.2 convergence acceptance contract became canonical.

    The release now converges encrypted private context, bounded computation, reconstruction accounting, cross-AI memory, scoped Grants, secretless actions, receipts and revocation behind one trust boundary with explicit release gates.

Current status

v1.2 convergence in progress. Hush is a user-owned trust layer, not only a private-computation kernel.

The repository now includes encrypted context, bounded private computation, cumulative reconstruction accounting, approval-gated cross-AI memory, scoped Grants, privacy-aware routing, secretless action brokerage, receipts, ciphertext-only sync and browser/desktop/mobile client work. Remaining release gates include OS-backed key handling, zero-terminal onboarding, a stable external protocol, the consumer trust surface, production multi-device revocation hardening, semantic/end-to-end leakage evaluation and independent reproduction.

Research status is deliberately split by evidence type: the 4,000-task protocol is Hush-authored and synthetic; AgentCIBench is an independently originated frozen holdout; AgentLeak is an independent detector over Hush-generated traces; v1f is development-selected.
