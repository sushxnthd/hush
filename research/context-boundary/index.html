Measuring a private context boundary.

Frozen AgentCIBench v1 · n = 50Full protocol and results ↗

The method

Four policies were evaluated on the same 50 frozen external cases: raw context, semantic-only retrieval, lexical minimization and Hush CI. Cases cover recipient misalignment, task ambiguity and visual co-location.

Results

Hush CI reduces mean violation by 15.4% relative to semantic-only retrieval. Six Hush cases were leak-free; none were leak-free for the matched semantic baseline. Substantial exposure remains.

The claim boundary

This supports an internal contextual-integrity result on this frozen holdout. It does not establish universal privacy, named-commercial-competitor superiority or independent reproduction.

Reproduce and inspect

Read the complete linked report for protocol definitions, statistical analysis, commit identifiers and evaluation receipts. The authors of Hush designed and ran this experiment.
