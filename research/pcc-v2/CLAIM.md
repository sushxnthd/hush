# PCC v2 Claim Boundary

## Supported

On the tested POLAR structured-context regime, PCC v2 can preserve deterministic benchmark utility while eliminating observed exact protected-value disclosure by replacing raw private values with policy-authorized task facts and abstractions.

The untouched P2 confirmation covered 1,570 cases and passed all preregistered gates.

## Not supported

- universal semantic privacy
- zero information leakage under arbitrary inference
- arbitrary unstructured-text transformation
- resistance to every side channel
- independent third-party reproduction
- universal superiority to commercial privacy or agent systems
- proof that trusted local schema labels can always be obtained correctly

Any public or paper claim must preserve these boundaries.
