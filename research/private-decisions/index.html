The method

The internal evaluation ran 4,000 paired synthetic tasks across travel, shopping, scheduling and job selection, using 20 deterministic seeds and 50 profiles per family per seed. Utility is agreement with the task oracle, not open-ended answer quality.

The Hush path matched the oracle on every task and returned zero exact private input attributes. In the adaptive threshold reconstruction experiment, the unprotected path recovered every tested secret; Hush resisted the tested identity-rotation and profile-revision reset attacks.

The claim boundary

The tasks are synthetic and exactly representable by Hush’s decision language. The attacks cover tested adaptive threshold families. Timing, traffic, compromised-host and process-memory side channels are outside this experiment. This does not prove universal or named-commercial-competitor superiority.
