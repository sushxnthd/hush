Mission

Personal AI should not require surrendering a personal profile.

The path to useful personal AI is to move computation toward the user, rather than moving every private fact toward the model.

Models increasingly need calendars, finances, preferences, credentials, health context, work history and real-world authority. Supakeep makes those resources usable without making raw disclosure the default interface.

What we do

A trust layer outside the model.

01

Blind personalization.

Agents send public candidates and bounded decision programs. Supakeep evaluates them locally against private context and returns only the useful result.

↗
02

Trajectory privacy.

Disclosure is accounted across the entire task and across delegated agents, so many individually harmless queries cannot silently compose into an exact secret.

↗
03

Scoped authority.

Signed Grants, exact-action approvals, MCP enforcement and brokered credentials let an agent act without receiving blanket control of the user's digital life.

↗
