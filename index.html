THE PROBLEM

Smarter agents need more access.

What AI knows

People already share identity, relationships, finances, health concerns, company information and credentials with AI. Supakeep is designed to minimize what leaves the trusted boundary.

What AI does

As agents send messages, edit files, make purchases and operate accounts, access control becomes part of product safety. Supakeep evaluates those actions outside the model.
