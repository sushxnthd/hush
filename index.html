Works with your AI

The path to useful AI starts with context that belongs to you, across any model, any conversation. Your preferences, history and decisions should carry forward without handing over your entire private life.

A new chat should not mean a new introduction. And a more capable assistant should not mean giving up control over what it knows or what it can do.

Hush keeps private notes, deliberate memory and a boundary you can inspect in a user-owned workspace.

Start with encrypted notes in your browser. Approve what becomes memory. Review exactly what leaves. The local runtime extends this idea with bounded computation, scoped permissions and signed receipts.

What we do

Memory you choose.
Turn a useful fact or preference into a proposed memory. Approve, reject or forget it. Only approved memories become selectable context for your next AI session.
Any AI, on your terms.
Keep your context outside the chat window. Prepare a reviewed prompt for the AI you choose, or connect supported agents to the local runtime through MCP.
Permission stays with you.
The local runtime separates an agent’s proposal from scoped authority, exact-action approval and an auditable outcome. Your private facts stay behind a deliberate boundary.

The browser workspace works today, without an account. Local connectors require setup; signed native distribution remains release-gated. Choose your starting point.
