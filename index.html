How it works

AI asks Hush. Hush answers with only what the task needs.

Your files, calendar, messages, preferences and credentials stay behind a user-owned boundary. The model gets a bounded result, approved memory or scoped action — not a standing copy of the raw data.

What Hush does

Three primitives, one trust layer.

Hush is not another memory database. It decides what AI may know, what it may remember, and what it may do.

YOU

Private context that stays private.

Hush resolves semantic requests locally and can compute over selected connector fields without exposing the underlying record, private path or unrelated values to the model.

One memory, across models.

Any AI can propose a memory, but only you can make it canonical. Approved state lives in Hush rather than being trapped inside one provider, so continuity can move with you.

KEYOK

Authority without handing over secrets.

Agents can request scoped actions while credentials stay brokered outside the model-facing surface. Exact approvals, one-shot tickets and receipts keep the authority narrow and inspectable.
