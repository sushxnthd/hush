The inversion

Move the decision to your data.

Personal AI normally works by moving your personal context into a model. Supakeep reverses the direction: the model brings a bounded decision request to your private context.

Today
Your profile→AI provider→Answer

Deep personalization requires repeatedly copying more of you into somebody else’s system.

With Supakeep
AI decision→Your private context→Bounded result

The AI can still make a personalized choice while your underlying profile remains local and provider-neutral.
