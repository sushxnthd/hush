BRING CONTEXT TO

Every new chat starts by asking you to explain yourself again. The more useful AI becomes, the more of your life you are asked to hand over.

Your context should belong to you.

Hush is building the user-owned layer between you and your AI tools: a place to keep context, approve memory and set the boundary around what an assistant can know and do.

Why we’re building Hush ↗
THE PRODUCT

Useful AI. A deliberate boundary.

01

A home for your context.

Your notes and memories should outlast the chat window. Store them privately, approve what is remembered and take selected context to the AI you choose.

02

Personal, without the full profile.

The browser workspace prepares only selected, approved memory. The local runtime can compute bounded decisions against private state without releasing the underlying facts.

03

Permission is a decision.

A useful assistant should still ask. Hush’s local runtime separates an agent’s proposal from scoped authority, exact-action approval and an auditable outcome.
