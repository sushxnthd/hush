WHY SUPAKEEP

AI trust is fragmented.

Your context is scattered.

Different assistants remember different pieces of your identity, finances, relationships, work and preferences. Supakeep is designed to make that exposure visible across providers.

Your authority is scattered.

Password managers, OAuth, MCP tools and operating systems each control one piece. Supakeep sits above them and expresses the human rule: what this agent may know and do for this purpose.
