hush·Scope & policies

Your context belongs to you.

Updated October 8, 2026Hush support ↗

This notice covers the website, browser workspace, sample walkthrough and local runtime.

A website, without a tracking layer.

Hush does not add account signup, analytics scripts, advertising trackers or tracking cookies to this site. Fonts, scripts and media are served locally with the site. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process request metadata such as IP addresses under GitHub’s privacy statement.

Encrypted at rest. Local by default.

Your notes, memories and activity are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before being saved to IndexedDB. A non-exportable key is derived from your passphrase using PBKDF2-SHA256 with a random salt and 600,000 iterations. The passphrase is not stored or sent to a Hush server. While unlocked, plaintext and the key are available to this page in memory.

Sample mode is temporary and does not save a workspace. The browser app has no model API calls or cloud synchronization. Locking clears the app’s working state; inactivity triggers a lock after 15 minutes. A compromised device, browser extension or malicious same-origin script remains a risk.

You choose when context leaves.

Copying puts the reviewed text on your system clipboard. Clipboard managers may retain it. Pasting into another service shares that text under the service’s own policies. Hush does not send or verify the paste. Pattern detection can miss private data.

Backups contain an encrypted snapshot of your notes, memories and activity. Importing decrypts locally after you supply the original passphrase, then replaces this browser’s workspace only after confirmation. Older backups can retain deleted records. Hush has no recovery service or passphrase reset.

A separate same-device store.

The runtime binds to loopback and uses per-user storage. Its root key uses supported OS keystores; private context and credential values are encrypted. Some operational metadata has different protections. Supported connectors and actions communicate with providers you configure. Local-first does not mean every connected workflow is offline.

Use the runtime’s documented controls to disconnect providers, revoke permissions or remove local records. Review the runtime privacy notice and threat model for details.

Removal and questions.

Removing a browser note or forgetting a memory removes it from the active workspace. To erase the entire saved workspace, clear this site’s browser storage after preserving any backup you need. This is irreversible without a usable backup and passphrase. External services and prior backups may retain their own copies.
