Claim boundaries

The strongest evidence is on explicit output channels under controlled workloads. Timing, compromised hosts, bypass traffic, network metadata, covert channels and unmediated side effects remain outside the guarantee. Development-selected mechanisms do not become confirmatory claims until they survive a fresh untouched evaluation.
